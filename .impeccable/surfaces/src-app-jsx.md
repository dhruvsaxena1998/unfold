# Surface: Stretch timer (single screen)

Scope: the whole app, one screen. Mode: Operate.
Audience/job: one person rehabbing a slipped disc; start hold, stop, rest, repeat. Viewed from a mat on phone or laptop, 1–2 m away.
Constraints: count-up hold primary; rest auto-tracked secondary; session total; optional target with soft beep/vibration; flat rep list; no persistence; spacebar + whole-screen tap.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The hold is a flood tide rising up a painted gauge staff; rest is slack water. Refuses the category default of a dark stopwatch ring with a lap list.

OWN-WORLD: Harbour-water teal field (#0f3b40 deep, lighter surface band), enamel staff white (#e9ece6) with black graduation ticks and red E-mark metre numerals (#c8322b), ink #10181c. Tabular condensed numerals. Almanac tide-table grid for the log. Fixed regions never move between states. Every figure wears a label.

STORY: I see at a glance whether I am holding or resting, for how long, and my session total; one tap or space flips hold/rest; I can glance at the tide table of reps afterwards.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Left/centre: a vertical enamel gauge staff full height with ticks; water rises behind it as the hold progresses (towards target if set, else one minute per full staff). Giant hold numeral (≈30vh) on the staff face. Right margin column: REST and SESSION figures labelled, target control. Whole screen is the tap target; a labelled bar at the bottom reads "Tap or Space: start hold". Log (tide table with stepped strips) below the fold / side panel on desktop.
